## Evidence Capture & Storage Framework

### Strategic Evidence Requirements

#### C-Suite Decision Evidence
- **Board Presentation Quality**: Video recordings of AI-generated presentations with board simulation feedback
- **Strategic Decision Audit Trail**: Complete decision provenance with reasoning, alternatives considered, and outcome tracking
- **ROI Realization Documentation**: Financial impact measurements with third-party validation
- **Regulatory Compliance Reports**: Continuous compliance monitoring with automated regulatory reporting
- **Stakeholder Satisfaction Metrics**: Quarterly surveys across executive team with trend analysis

#### VP-Level Operational Evidence
- **P&L Responsibility Tracking**: Automated financial performance monitoring with budget variance analysis
- **Cross-functional Coordination Metrics**: Meeting effectiveness, decision speed, alignment scores
- **Operational Efficiency Improvements**: Productivity metrics, cost reduction validation, process optimization
- **Talent Management Outcomes**: Retention rates, performance improvements, career progression tracking

### Implementation Evidence Requirements

#### Technical Performance Evidence
- **Code Quality vs FAANG Standards**: Automated comparison against Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft code quality benchmarks
- **System Reliability Metrics**: 99.99% uptime validation with error budget tracking
- **Security Assessment Results**: Third-party penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, compliance audits
- **Innovation Pipeline Outputs**: Patent applications, technology advancement tracking, R&D ROI measurement

#### Business Intelligence Evidence
- **Market Forecast Accuracy**: 6-month rolling accuracy tracking with variance analysis
- **Competitive Intelligence Validation**: Actionability scoring, strategic recommendation adoption rates
- **Financial Model Precision**: ±10% variance validation with actual outcome comparison
- **Strategic Advisory Impact**: Board acceptance rates, strategy implementation success, business outcome correlation

### Compliance Evidence Requirements

#### Regulatory Documentation
- **EU AI Act Compliance**: High-risk system classification documentation, transparency requirements, human oversight evidence
- **ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS**: Complete AI management system documentation with continuous improvement tracking
- **NIST CSF 2.0 Implementation**: Tier 4 Adaptive framework evidence with continuous monitoring
- **SOC 2 Type II Evidence**: Annual attestation preparation with control effectiveness documentation

#### Audit Trail Requirements
- **Decision Provenance**: Complete AI decision traceability with reasoning explanation and human oversight points
- **Change Management**: All system modifications with approval workflows, impact assessment, and rollback procedures
- **Access Control**: Role-based access with privilege escalation tracking and approval audit trails
- **Incident Management**: Complete incident lifecycle with response times, resolution procedures, and lessons learned

### Monitoring and Alerting Framework

#### Critical Threshold Monitoring
- **Performance Degradation**: >10% variance from baseline triggers immediate investigation
- **Compliance Violations**: Any regulatory non-compliance triggers legal review and remediation
- **Financial Variance**: >15% budget variance triggers CFO escalation and corrective action
- **Quality Regression**: Code quality below enterprise standards triggers automatic rollback

#### Escalation Procedures
- **Level 1**: Automated system response with self-healing procedures
- **Level 2**: AI system supervisor notification with corrective action recommendations  
- **Level 3**: Human supervisor escalation with decision override capabilities
- **Level 4**: Executive escalation with board notification for strategic impacts

#### Continuous Improvement Loop
- **Monthly Evidence Review**: Comprehensive analysis of all evidence categories with trend identification
- **Quarterly Validation Audit**: Third-party validation of evidence integrity and accuracy
- **Annual Compliance Assessment**: Full regulatory compliance review with certification updates
- **Continuous Optimization**: Evidence collection process improvements based on regulatory feedback

This comprehensive risk and evidence framework ensures complete organizational replacement systems maintain enterprise governance, regulatory compliance, and business outcome validation required for Fortune 500 adoption and unicorn-level investment justification.
